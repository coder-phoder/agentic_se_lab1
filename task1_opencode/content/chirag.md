## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI tools save me a lot of time for small things like tests and short scripts. But in big projects it forgets the context and starts guessing. I use it like a fast intern. Helpful, but I check everything.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Coding will be more like managing AI agents than typing code yourself. Planning and design will be more important than syntax. Maybe "AI code debugger" will be a real job.

## My hot take on AI
Prompt engineering is just writing clear requirements, and we were always bad at that.
