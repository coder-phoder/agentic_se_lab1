## What I think about AI in software engineering today
I use AI a lot to understand new libraries and error messages, and it helps a lot. But it sounds very sure even when it is wrong, so I always check the documentation. I think the danger is that students stop learning the basics.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
People will build simple apps just by describing them in normal language. Testing and checking data will become a bigger part of the job. Understanding the problem will be more important than knowing one framework.

## My hot take on AI
If you can't explain the code the AI wrote, then you didn't build it. You just copied a guess.
