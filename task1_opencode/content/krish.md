## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI is really good for simple and repeated code. It can make a basic MERN app in a few minutes. But sometimes it uses functions that don't even exist, so I always have to check its code. For me it is a helper, not a replacement.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
I think AI agents will write most of the code. Developers will spend more time checking, testing and deciding what to build. The important skill will be knowing if the code is actually correct.

## My hot take on AI
AI will not take your job, but a junior with 3 AI agents and no code review can break production.
