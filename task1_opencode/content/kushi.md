## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI is great for quick prototypes. I can go from an idea to a working UI in one afternoon. But the designs often look the same and a bit boring. AI does the first 80 percent, but the last 20 percent still needs a human.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Making software will be very fast, so good ideas and good user experience will matter most. Small teams with AI will do what big teams do today. I hope design and accessibility finally get more attention.

## My hot take on AI
AI will make every app look the same, so good design will be the most valuable skill in tech.
